DialOS M0 — Operator Transparency & Hardware Verification Guide

Exact walkthrough of every command you will run on your Mac, every file touched on your microSD card, and every screen you will see on your FiiO Snowsky Disc (V2.57) during M0.

SD-Card Non-Destruction & Recovery Guarantees
  • No USB re-flash needed for M0: Your Disc already has the microSD hot-reload hook. Deploying M0 only copies one binary to <SDCARD>/.dial-os/mq_ui.
  • Music files are strictly read-only: ./dialos-mac sd-update writes atomically to .dial-os/mq_ui (temp file + fsync + rename) and never deletes, formats, or modifies any other file or folder.
  • Stock rm -rf %s bug neutralized: Whenever ./dialos-mac build repacks a V2.57 NAND image, it automatically patches mq_player's dangerous mount_sdcard() rm -rf %s string to rmdir %s and fails the build if the patch is not verified.
  • On-device power-off safety: The M0 spike sends 0664=0 on boot so mq_player can never trigger an unsynced poweroff -f while logs are open, and calls fsync() after every stage.
  • 5-second instant escape back to stock FiiO UI: Run ./dialos-mac use-stock (creates empty <SDCARD>/.dial-os/use_stock) and reboot. Your Disc boots stock /usr/bin/mq_ui.stock immediately.

1. What You Do on Your Mac (Build & Deploy — ~10 Seconds)

Step A — Verify & Build

Run Mac tests & cross-compile M0 spike

make test-tools
./dialos-mac doctor
make m0-spike

What you will see:

  • test-tools passes all Python unit tests (DGSB plan, OpenSSL decrypt, ELF EF_MIPS_NAN2008 patch, mq_player rmdir safety check, atomic SD deploy).
  • doctor confirms zig, all 5 vendor binaries, vendor/stock_v257/rootfs.squashfs, and your mounted microSD card.
  • make m0-spike builds build/m0_mq_ui for MIPS32r2 FP64A NAN2008.
Step B — Stage 2 Test Tracks + Binary

Copy binary to microSD (Zero music touched)

./dialos-mac sd-update --binary build/m0_mq_ui

Test audio tracks: Your real 24/48 FLAC tracks work great! Put 2 tracks in <SDCARD>/.dial-os/m0_test/ (or skip this and the spike safely picks the first 2 audio files already on your card, read-only). We can also optionally generate a 20 s synthetic 24/192 FLAC tone on Mac if you want peak-load stress testing.

Exact files created on SD:

  • <SDCARD>/.dial-os/mq_ui (only file written by Mac)
Physical Setup Before Powering On
  • Eject the microSD card safely from macOS and insert it into the Snowsky Disc.
  • Plug in your wired headphones (Bluetooth stays OFF for all of M0).
  • Power on the Disc. The hot-reload hook stages .dial-os/mq_ui and launches the M0 Runner.

3. Exact Screens You Will See on the Disc (5 Stages)

Each stage runs on the 360×360 round screen, automatically saves its numbers to /tmp/sdcard/.dial-os/m0_report.txt and raw IPC frames to /tmp/sdcard/.dial-os/m0_ipc_trace.log, and calls fsync() before turning green.

Stage 1: 360x360 Disc + Tear Bar
Stage 1 of 5

360×360 Fixed-Point Rotation & Tearing

What you do: Confirm your wired 24/192 FLAC track is playing in your headphones (tap Play if paused), then tap RUN. Watch the red vertical bar sweep across the spinning disc for 10 seconds (5 s single-page direct blit, then 5 s triple-buffer FBIOPAN_DISPLAY).

What to watch/listen for: Does the red bar split horizontally (tearing) in either mode? Do you hear any crackle or audio drop in your headphones?

Pass gate: >= 30 fps with zero audio stutters. If < 30 fps, M3b uses static cover art + rotating progress ring.

Stage 2: Buttons Hidden While Tracing
Stage 2 of 5

cst816t Rim Touch Sample Rate

What you do: Tap RUN, then trace your thumb continuously around the full dashed amber outer rim ring for 10 seconds. Both RUN and NEXT disappear and are completely ignored during the 10-second countdown so your finger can sweep all the way around the bottom rim without accidentally pressing anything. When the timer hits 0 s, the Result Summary appears and the buttons return.

On-screen numbers & pass gate: SAMPLES, HZ (target >= 60 Hz), MAX GAP ms (target <= 50 ms), DROPOUTS, and MIN/MAX (x, y).

Stage 3: Automated Track Handoffs
Stage 3 of 5

Rolling-Slice Playback Handoff

What you do: Tap RUN and listen for ~15 seconds. The spike automatically switches between your two test tracks using alternating 0602 M3U files (A/B) and probes CUSTOM_PLAYLIST / folder handoff.

What it logs: Switch latency from TX to first advancing a103 position tick, any rate-limit drops, and whether mq_player ever falls back to stock song.db.

Decision: Proves rolling-slice handoff or triggers Plan B (pass full active album/playlist/folder, slice All songs only above threshold).

Stage 4: V2.57 EQ/PEQ Write Test
Stage 4 of 5

V2.57 EQ / PEQ Acceptance Probe

What you do: Tap RUN and listen for ~10 seconds. The spike sends a fixed EQ preset (0508), a graphic EQ frame (filterType: 0), and a custom PEQ probe, then restores EQ to flat/OFF.

What it logs: Every exact `/ui` reply frame so we know with certainty whether V2.57 accepts or ignores PEQ writes.

Stage 5: Golden Trace + Final Sync
Stage 5 of 5

Golden V2.57 IPC Trace (Q14 / Q17 / Q18)

What you do: Tap RUN and wait ~20 seconds while the spike plays a track, pauses/resumes, seeks to 95%, seeks to 98%, and lets the track finish naturally to EOF. When ALL 5 STAGES SYNCED! appears, tap DONE, then power off the Disc.

What it logs: Every timestamped raw /player TX and /ui RX frame in /tmp/sdcard/.dial-os/m0_ipc_trace.log — the exact golden replay fixture for the M1 Mac simulator.
